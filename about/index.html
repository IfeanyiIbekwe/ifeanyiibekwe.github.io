About Ify

I turn brand into revenue, across luxury industries that have traditionally treated the two as separate jobs.

I lead brand and performance marketing for Jumeirah Marsa Al Arab, ranked among The World’s 50 Best Hotels 2025. I own the full marketing function across rooms, resort, and spa, with direct commercial accountability for the property’s largest revenue line. I lead a team of two and direct our media partner, MAGNA, as the client principal.

My remit spans both halves of the discipline that usually live in different people. On the brand side, I own end-to-end delivery of the property’s brand campaign, from talent and production into offline activations alongside the PR team, through to the landing pages and media buy. On the performance side, I plan and execute across Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, programmatic, and Google, aligning funnel stages to landing-page and conversion logic. The throughline of my career has been scaling marketing into revenue in luxury, at progressively larger scope: from building e-commerce platforms from the ground up, to digital lead at Madinat Jumeirah, where I drove 15% YoY growth across 20+ dining venues, to full marketing ownership of a flagship property.

What makes my version of this distinctive is that I also build the commerce. Alongside my career I co-founded The Business of Preloved Fashion (BOPF), a luxury resale platform, where I own the full technical stack from storefront to checkout. Integrating the systems behind a real transaction — payments, CRM, and applied AI among them — means I don’t theorize about where marketing strategy meets the platform that executes it. I’ve built both, so I lead both without a translation layer.

I own the brand story, the demand engine, and the platform beneath both, and I build each one to convert.
