      Strength plan — 3 days a week

      In a big calorie deficit, lifting is what makes the weight you lose fat instead of muscle. Three sessions a week, at least a day of rest between them. Form guide with pictures below.

      Day A — Legs
      • Squat — 3 × 10
      • Lunges — 3 × 10 each leg
      • Plank — 3 × 30 seconds
      • Treadmill incline walk — 15 min
      Day B — Push
      • Bench press — 3 × 10
      • Overhead press — 3 × 10
      • Plank — 3 × 30 seconds
      • Treadmill easy jog — 10 min
      Day C — Pull
      • Deadlift — 3 × 8
      • Pull-ups (or lat pulldown) — 3 × as many as you can
      • One-arm dumbbell row — 3 × 10 each side
      • Treadmill intervals — 10 min (1 min brisk / 1 min easy)

      Start light enough to finish every set with clean form. Progress by adding one rep per session; at 12 reps, add weight and drop back to 8–10. Log each session under "Strength training" on the Today tab.

      Exercise form guide

      Figures animate between the start and finish of each rep. The body diagram shows the muscles the exercise works (highlighted in blue).

      Squat
      • Feet shoulder-width, toes slightly out
      • Sit back and down until thighs are parallel
      • Chest up, heels flat, knees track over toes
      • Drive up through the heels
      Works: quads · glutes · core
      Deadlift
      • Bar over mid-foot, shins close to it
      • Push hips back, flat back — never rounded
      • Stand up by driving hips forward
      • Arms stay straight; the legs do the lifting
      Works: hamstrings · glutes · back
      Bench press
      • Eyes under the bar, feet flat on the floor
      • Lower to mid-chest with control
      • Press up until arms are straight
      • Wrists stacked over elbows
      Works: chest · triceps · shoulders
      Overhead press
      • Bar at shoulders, elbows under wrists
      • Brace your core — don't lean back
      • Press straight up past your face
      • Finish with biceps by your ears
      Works: shoulders · triceps · core
      Pull-up
      • Hands slightly wider than shoulders
      • Pull until your chin clears the bar
      • Lower slowly — that half builds muscle too
      • Too hard? Use the assisted machine or lat pulldown
      Works: back · biceps
      One-arm dumbbell row
      • One hand on the bench, back flat
      • Pull the weight to your hip, not your chest
      • Squeeze the shoulder blade at the top
      • No twisting — torso stays square
      Works: back · biceps
      Lunge
      • Big step forward, drop the back knee
      • Front knee at 90°, over the ankle
      • Torso upright, core tight
      • Push off the front heel to stand
      Works: quads · glutes · hamstrings
      Plank
      • Forearms down, elbows under shoulders
      • One straight line from head to heels — it's a hold, no movement
      • Squeeze glutes; don't let hips sag or pike
      • Breathe — don't hold your breath
      Works: core · shoulders · glutes
      Treadmill
      • Incline walk (10–12% at 4–5 km/h) burns like a jog, easier on knees
      • Intervals: 1 min brisk, 1 min easy, repeat
      • Don't hold the rails — it cancels the incline
      • Log it as Walking or Running on Today
      Works: quads · hamstrings · calves

      Intermittent fasting

      Fasting doesn't burn extra calories by itself — it works by shrinking the hours you can eat, which makes staying under budget easier (no late-night snacking). A common starting point is 16:8: eat between 12:00 and 20:00, water/black coffee/zobo (unsweetened) outside it.

      Set your window in Settings and the Today screen will show whether you're fasting or free to eat. If you train hard, keep protein high inside the window.

      Habits that decide the outcome

      1. Log everything, especially oil. A ladle of stew or fried-food oil can hide 200+ kcal. When unsure, round up.
      2. Protein at every meal — aim ~1.6 g per kg of goal weight (~120 g/day for a 75 kg goal): eggs, chicken, fish, beans, moi moi.
      3. 8–10k steps daily. It's the easiest 300–500 kcal you'll ever burn. Log them below the workouts.
      4. Weigh in every morning, after the bathroom, before eating. Judge the weekly trend, never a single day.
      5. Sleep 7+ hours. Short sleep measurably increases hunger the next day.
      6. Drinks count. A 50cl soft drink is ~210 kcal; water and zero-sugar drinks are 0.
