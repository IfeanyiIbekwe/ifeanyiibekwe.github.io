About

The throughline of my career has been scaling marketing into revenue in luxury, at progressively larger scope.

My remit spans both halves of the discipline that usually live in different people. On the brand side, I own end-to-end delivery of the property’s brand campaign, from talent and production into offline activations alongside the PR team, through to the landing pages and media buy. On the performance side, I plan and execute across Meta, TikTok, Snapchat, programmatic, and Google, aligning funnel stages to landing-page and conversion logic.

What makes my version of this distinctive is that I also build the commerce. Integrating the systems behind a real transaction — payments, CRM, and applied AI among them — means I don’t theorize about where marketing strategy meets the platform that executes it. I’ve built both, so I lead both without a translation layer.

I own the brand story, the demand engine, and the platform beneath both, and I build each one to convert.
