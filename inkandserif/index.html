Maison

A theme for restaurants.

A block theme for restaurants, built on core WordPress blocks: Light and Dark versions, palettes and type pairings chosen in WordPress's own Styles, and two demos to start from, Sable and Linnet.

Maison Core

The plugin behind it.

Menus, booking and opening hours as blocks and settings in WordPress, with the data search engines read. They live in the plugin, so a restaurant keeps them if it ever changes theme.

BOPF

Luxury, preloved.

An app for iOS and Android, product pages, a concierge chat, campaign art and emails for Business of Preloved Fashion, a luxury preloved boutique in Dubai.
