Maison guide

Version 1.0.0

How to set up Maison and make it your restaurant's site. The same guide is in WordPress, at Appearance > Maison > Guide, and in your download, as docs/guide.html. Appearance > Maison > Get started links straight to each place it mentions.

Maison needs WordPress 6.9 or later and PHP 7.4 or later.

Install

  1. Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme. Choose maison-theme-1.0.0.zip from your download, then Activate. (Upload that zip, not the whole download: WordPress refuses the download with "missing the style.css stylesheet".)
  2. Appearance > Maison > Get started. Click Install and activate Maison Core. The theme carries the plugin, so there is nothing else to upload. Maison Core adds the menus, booking, opening hours, recognition, the film opener and the demo import, and keeps them if you ever change theme.
  3. Import a demo, on the same page: one click (next section).

If your host asks for FTP details when you click Install, enter them, or upload maison-core-plugin-1.0.0.zip from your download at Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin instead.

The download also holds maison-child-theme-1.0.0.zip. You only need it if you or a developer will change the theme's files: upload it like the theme, then activate Maison Child instead of Maison. Your changes then survive updates.

Downloaded from Creative Market? That download comes without the font files (Creative Market's rules). On Appearance > Maison > Get started, click Install fonts once. Until then your site shows system fonts.

Import a demo

A demo builds a finished restaurant site from Maison's patterns: Home, Menus and the other pages, the menus with their dishes, opening hours, booking, recognition, a journal post and the header menu. Then you write your own words over it and add your own photos.

  • Sable: fine dining over coals, dark and light versions, a film opener.
  • Linnet: an all-day bakery and kitchen, light, in the Terracotta palette and the Classic fonts, with breakfast, lunch and bakery menus.

On Appearance > Maison > Get started, click Import the Sable demo or Import the Linnet demo. On a new site it also fills Restaurant settings and, for Linnet, applies its look.

Restaurant > Demo content has the same buttons with their options: keep your Restaurant settings, skip the demo's look, or keep your own front page. Importing never deletes anything; importing a demo again adds a second copy.

The photos are placeholders, and so is the picture at the top of Sable's story, set in the Watercolour style for a painting or photo of your restaurant: replace it with your own, or untick Show illustrations (Make it yours, below).

Your restaurant

Restaurant > Settings holds what the site shows about the restaurant. Blocks, the footer and search engines all read from here, so you type each thing once.

TabWhat it holds
DetailsName, cuisine, price range, address, map search, telephone, email, WhatsApp number, social profiles, and the page with your menus.
HoursOne or more services a day (lunch, dinner), each with opening, closing and last orders, plus special hours and closures with a note for guests. "Same as the day before" copies a day.
BookingHow guests book: here on your site with Maison Reservations (below), SevenRooms (venue ID), a booking page on another site (OpenTable, Resy, Tock, TheFork or any link), a provider's widget code, or no online booking (telephone, email and WhatsApp). Also the button text, and the Reserve bar on phones.
MenusThe allergen list (EU and UK 14, US 9, your own list, or off), dietary tags, the currency symbol and code, and a kitchen note.
ReviewsYour Google and Tripadvisor review links, the ratings you type in, and Tripadvisor's badge code.
Search enginesThe structured data that tells Google your hours, address and menus. Leave it on unless an SEO plugin already adds it.

Opening hours show as 12 or 24 hour times, and weeks start on the day set in Settings > General.

Menus and dishes

Dishes live on the Menus page, as blocks:

  • Menus shows several menus as tabs, with the allergen switch and a print button.
  • Menu is one menu: a la carte, tasting or wine, with its price note and an optional PDF link.
  • Menu section groups dishes, such as To begin.
  • Dish is a dish or drink: name, description, up to two prices (glass and bottle, say), dietary tags and the allergens it contains.

To change a dish, open Pages > Menus, click the dish and type. To add one, click the last dish in a section, then the + below it, and choose Dish. Its prices, tags and allergens are in the block settings on the right.

The Allergen table block lists every dish on the page against your allergen list. Print on the Menus page prints only the menu, ready to save as a PDF.

Reservations on your site

Maison Core can take bookings itself, with nothing to sign up for and no fees: in Restaurant > Settings > Booking, choose "Here on my site, with Maison Reservations".

It is one of the five choices there. A restaurant on SevenRooms, OpenTable or any other service picks that instead, and keeps it: every Reserve button then opens that service, the Reservation form block shows that service's button, and nothing of Maison Reservations shows or loads. Switching back and forth keeps each choice's settings.

  • Times come from Hours: every service, from opening to last orders (or an hour before closing when a service has no last orders), with the special hours and closures. Bookings stop at midnight.
  • Rules: how many guests may arrive at one time, how many in one service (or no limit), times every 15 or 30 minutes or every hour, the largest party that can book online (larger groups are asked to call or email), how many days ahead, and the least notice, in minutes.
  • Confirmation: guests send a request that you confirm or decline, or bookings are confirmed at once while there is room.
  • Where guests book: every Reserve button, the header's and the Reserve bar on phones included, opens the booking window. To show the form in a page instead, add the Reservation form block.
  • Restaurant > Reservations lists the requests waiting (their number shows in the menu) and the day sheet: each booking's time, guests, contact and note, with Confirm, Decline, Cancel and No-show. Bookings taken by telephone go in the form at the bottom, at any time and size. Editors and administrators can use it.
  • Emails: you get one for every booking, at the bookings email (or the email in Details). Guests get one when they book, and when you confirm, decline or cancel; the confirmation carries a calendar file. WordPress sends email through your host, and those often land in spam or never leave: install an SMTP plugin, such as WP Mail SMTP or FluentSMTP, and send its test email.
  • Maison Reservations does not do table plans, deposits, or text and WhatsApp messages. For those, choose SevenRooms or another provider.

Make it yours

Everything here happens in WordPress's own Site Editor (Appearance > Editor). Appearance > Maison > Get started > Make it yours links straight to each part.

Logo and site icon

  • WordPress 7.1 and later: Appearance > Editor > Identity holds the logo, the site name and the site icon.
  • WordPress 6.9: open Appearance > Editor > Patterns > Header, click the name in the header, then Replace to use your logo. The site icon is in Settings > General.

Maison's header works with a text name, a wide logo, or a square mark beside the name. For the dark version and over the opener, Appearance > Maison > Settings can use a second logo, or turn a one-colour logo white.

Colours

Appearance > Editor > Styles > Colors. Pick a palette: the first is Ivory, the theme's own, then Terracotta and Stone. Or click Palette to change any colour. Each colour has a light and a dark value, named with "(dark)", because the version can switch between them.

The version itself is in Appearance > Maison > Settings: Light, Dark, or Follow the visitor's device.

Fonts

Appearance > Editor > Styles > Typography. Pick a pairing under Typesets: the first is the theme's own (Newsreader and Albert Sans), then Classic, Couture and Pure.

Maison sets type in three roles, and a pairing sets all three:

  • Display: big headings, the site name, pull quotes and the menu on phones.
  • Text: small headings, dish names, quotes and italic accents.
  • Body: paragraphs, navigation, buttons, captions and forms.

To change one role, open Appearance > Maison > Settings and choose its font under Fonts: any of Maison's eight, or one of your own. A sample beside each shows the font. The change reaches every place the role is used, in the editor too.

Choosing a pairing in Styles later sets all three again. WordPress then also leaves your own fonts installed but inactive; they stay under Your fonts, and choosing one makes it active again.

To use a font of your own, add it to WordPress's font library first: Appearance > Fonts (WordPress 7.0 and later), or on WordPress 6.9 Appearance > Editor > Styles > Typography, then Manage fonts. Upload the font files, or pick one under Install Fonts, which copies a Google font to your site. It then shows under Your fonts in each role.

Fonts chosen for Headings or Text in Styles change only those elements, not dish names, quotes or the menu on phones; the roles change them all.

Maison's fonts load from your own site, never from another one.

Pictures and words

Open a page (Pages, then the page), click any text to write over it, and click a picture, then Replace, to use your own. Media uploads photos first if you prefer.

  • The opener at the top of Home is a Cover block: replace its picture the same way. For a film, see "The film opener" below.
  • Galleries take several photos at once: click the gallery, then Add.
  • Two image styles suit artwork: Drawing on paper for line drawings, and Watercolour for paintings, whose edges fade into the page. Click the image, then Styles in the block settings.
  • No painting or photo for the story? Appearance > Maison > Settings, untick Show illustrations: every Watercolour image hides at once, and comes back when you tick it again.

Sections and pages

Maison's patterns are ready-made sections. On any page, click +, then Patterns, then Restaurant sections: story, signature dishes, chef, quotes, galleries, private dining, events, gift card, newsletter, Instagram grid, seating plan and more. Restaurant openers and Restaurant pages (Home, About, Private dining) are there too.

For a new page, Pages > Add New, then choose a page pattern when WordPress offers them.

Header, footer and announcement bar

  • Appearance > Editor > Patterns lists the template parts: Header, Header (centred), Header (over the opener), Footer and Announcement bar. Click one to edit it.
  • To use another header on a template, open the template in Appearance > Editor > Templates, click the header, then the three dots in its toolbar, then Replace.
  • The announcement bar shows the text you type in it, with a close button. Empty it to hide the bar.
  • The header menu: Appearance > Editor > Navigation.

Additional CSS

Appearance > Editor > Styles, then Additional CSS (in the Styles panel, or under the three dots at its top, by WordPress version). Your CSS applies everywhere and survives theme updates. For example:

.wp-block-site-title { letter-spacing: 0.2em; }

The three font roles are CSS variables you can use, and they follow the fonts you choose: --wp--preset--font-family--display, --wp--preset--font-family--text and --wp--preset--font-family--body. For bigger changes, use the Maison Child theme.

Light, dark, grain and illustrations

Appearance > Maison > Settings:

  • Version: Light, Dark, or Follow the visitor's device. Visitors get no switch of their own.
  • Paper grain: a faint texture on the light version.
  • Illustrations: show or hide every Watercolour image.
  • Logo on dark backgrounds: a second logo, or a one-colour logo turned white.
  • Fonts: the font for each role (Fonts, above).

The film opener

The opener on Sable's Home plays a short silent film behind or beside the headline, with a pause button, and shows its poster first. Phones get a smaller film, or the poster.

  1. Open the page, click the opener, then Replace and choose your film (MP4).
  2. In the block settings, under Film: choose a phone video, and if you like, Add a Watch the film button, which opens a longer film with sound: a file, or a YouTube or Vimeo link, with optional captions.

How to prepare the films (length, size, sound, settings for HandBrake and FFmpeg): docs/film-guide.md in your download.

Recognition

Restaurant > Recognition holds awards, press quotes and guest reviews. Add award, press quote or review, then choose its kind. The Accolades block shows awards; the Quotes block shows press quotes and guest reviews. You choose what to show; nothing comes from a live feed.

Award logos. Open an award and set its Logo or artwork in the sidebar. A PNG or WebP with a transparent background shows best; use only logos you are allowed to display. Logos show whole, in their own shape. The Accolades block's Logo colours shows them As uploaded, or In the text colour, which follows the Light and Dark versions (a dark logo would otherwise vanish on Dark; this needs a transparent background).

Journal, events and forms

  • Posts are the Journal: news, events and recipes. Linnet's demo shows the Journal page.
  • Events are Journal posts in an Events category. SevenRooms users can show its events widget (Restaurant > Settings > Booking).
  • Forms: install Contact Form 7 (free) and add its form to a page; Maison styles it. Other form plugins get Maison's general form styles.

Map, reviews and Instagram

  • The Map block shows a Google map only when a visitor taps it, so pages stay fast and nothing goes to Google before then. No API key needed. It uses the address, or Map search, from Restaurant > Settings > Details.
  • Review links: Review us on Google and Tripadvisor, with your ratings.
  • Instagram grid: a pattern with your own photos and a follow link. For a live feed, the free Smash Balloon Instagram plugin drops into the same place with matching styles.

Search engines and speed

  • Maison Core tells search engines your address, hours, menus and awards (Restaurant > Settings > Search engines).
  • It also gives every page a description for search results, and a preview for links shared in WhatsApp, Messages or on social media: the page's title, its words and its picture. A page's words are its Excerpt (in the page's sidebar, under the title); without one, your tagline and hours (Settings > General > Tagline). A post uses its opening words. The picture is the page's or post's featured image, else its opener, else the home page's opener, so every shared link shows your restaurant.
  • With an SEO plugin (Yoast SEO, Rank Math and the like), Maison leaves the descriptions and previews to it on its own, and says so on Search engines; switch them off there if you prefer.
  • Fonts load from your site; the map and films wait for the visitor; the opener's poster paints first. Add a caching plugin on your host, and keep photos under about 2000 pixels wide.

Translation

Maison and Maison Core are ready for translation: their .pot files are in each one's languages folder. A translation plugin such as Loco Translate works directly with them.

Updating

Upload the new version's theme zip, such as maison-theme-1.1.0.zip (Appearance > Themes > Add New > Upload Theme), and its plugin zip, and choose Replace current with uploaded. Your pages, settings, menus and Styles stay. Changes to the theme's files are lost on update unless they are in the Maison Child theme.

When something is not right

The demo import says to activate Maison first. The demo is made from Maison's patterns: activate Maison (or Maison Child), then import.

Maison Core asks for FTP details, or will not install. Upload maison-core-plugin-1.0.0.zip at Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin instead.

Pages show "not found" after the import. Visit Settings > Permalinks and click Save once.

The Reserve button goes nowhere. Set how guests book in Restaurant > Settings > Booking. Without a Reservations page, the header button opens your booking directly.

Booking emails do not arrive. Install an SMTP plugin, such as WP Mail SMTP or FluentSMTP, and send its test email. Every booking is on Restaurant > Reservations, even when an email fails.

The booking window shows no times. Check that Hours has a service on that day and that the date is within the days ahead. Security plugins that close the REST API to visitors also stop the form: allow the maison/v1 routes.

The fonts look plain. In a Creative Market download, click Install fonts on Appearance > Maison > Get started. If it says Google Fonts cannot be reached, ask your host to allow fonts.gstatic.com, or try again later.

I changed a colour and the dark version did not follow. Dark values are separate colours, named "(dark)", in the same palette.

Something else. Note your WordPress and PHP versions (Tools > Site Health > Info) and what you clicked, and write to ify@thebopf.com.
