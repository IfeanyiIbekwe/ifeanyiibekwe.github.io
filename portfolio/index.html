Business of Preloved Fashion (BOPF)

Co-founded a luxury resale platform in 2021 and own the full technical build as sole technical decision-maker — scoped, built, integrated, and maintained alongside a full-time marketing leadership role.

The constraint

No engineering team. A live, revenue-generating business with real customers and no room for downtime. Every build happens under genuine cost, time, and reliability pressure — this is applied engineering on a working platform, not experimentation.

The stack

A Shopify commerce core integrating roughly a dozen systems — payments, BNPL, email/SMS, CRM, consignment management, and advertising — into one resilient operation, from storefront to checkout.

The AI layer
  • AI-powered integrations connecting the CMS, consignment, and marketplace systems, automating previously manual listing and data work across platforms.
  • In-house WhatsApp and email marketing tools, built to replace paid third-party tooling and eliminate recurring usage fees.
  • A customer-facing AI concierge on the platform.
  • Automation agents supporting day-to-day operations.
  • An AI generated-media pipeline for producing marketing and product media.
The judgment

Beyond the individual tools: solving undocumented integration problems, sequencing the build roadmap by dependency and risk, and making every build-or-buy and infrastructure tradeoff against real cost, real customers, and real constraint.

Key Tools: Shopify, Systems Integration, Applied AI, Payments & BNPL, CRM, Meta Ads, Google Ads
